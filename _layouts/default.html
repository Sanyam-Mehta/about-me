Research

My current research with Dr. Andrew Owens explores the surprising, emergent capabilities of large generative models.

Tracking Emerges in Video Diffusion Models

(Submitted to NeurIPS 2025)

What if a model designed to create videos could also be used to understand them? [cite_start]We discovered that powerful, off-the-shelf video diffusion models have a hidden talent for tracking objects without any specialized training[cite: 19]. [cite_start]Our method is pretty straightforward: we place a colored dot on an object in the first frame of a video and prompt the model to regenerate the video, making sure the dot sticks to the object[cite: 20]. [cite_start]The trick is a new guidance technique we developed called "Marker Enhancement Guidance" that encourages the model to keep the marker consistent across all the frames[cite: 21, 280]. [cite_start]This zero-shot approach works surprisingly well, outperforming many previous zero-shot methods and proving competitive with specialized self-supervised models[cite: 23].

Why Robotics?

For People: My time at Gather AI opened my eyes to the real-world impact of robotics. I learned that in 2021, nearly 38% of accidental deaths in warehouses were due to slips, trips, and falls. Seeing our autonomous drones collect data from dangerous, hard-to-reach places—keeping people safely on the ground—showed me that robotics isn't just about efficiency; it's about protecting lives.

For the Planet: I was heartbroken to learn about the devastation wildfires have caused in California, destroying 20% of the world's Giant Sequoias—trees that are thousands of years old. I imagine a future where advanced, accessible robots can assist rangers in protecting these natural wonders from fires and other threats. This is the kind of work I dream of contributing to.

For the Fun of It: And sometimes, the reason is simple: robots are just cool. I love the challenge of building things, and the idea of creating intelligent machines that can help people is what gets me excited to learn and create every single day.
